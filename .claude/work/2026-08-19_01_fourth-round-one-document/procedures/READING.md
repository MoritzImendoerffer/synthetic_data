## The rule, fixed in `state.json → decisions.pass_rule` before the new document was written

PASS iff the owner judges the new document the better text **and** quotes fewer than five sentences
from it across whatever was read. Anything else is FAIL. Applied mechanically after the reading is
recorded verbatim and the key opened.

pdfs) → count the quoted sentences per source → apply the rule → settle D6. Never the other way.
