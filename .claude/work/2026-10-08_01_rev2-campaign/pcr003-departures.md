# Departures PCR-003 must record against PCP-003 Version 2.0 (from the PCP-003 author, 2026-10-09)

PCP-003 Version 2.0 keeps every Version 1.0 rule. Against them, PCR-003 records (as deviations where a
rule or procedure of the approved plan was departed from, as discrepancies where the plan did not foresee
the work; never silently):
- osmolality was active in the screening but not carried into the response-surface design, and the
  design was not amended before execution (the later S-BRX-OSM is PD-003-01);
- the NOR was not narrowed although acidic variants fail at the worst NOR corner (owner's decision 6a:
  accepted as the producer's risk, with mitigation);
- the univariate effect of initial viable cell concentration on acidic variants (the rule sends an effect
  the mechanism does not explain to a multivariate study);
- host cell protein, DNA, viable cell density, viability and titre were not measured as the plan requires;
- the univariate study started before the response-surface study (plan sequence);
- the univariate runs were replicated (not in the plan);
- probe drift DEV-003-01 against the calibration verification before and after execution;
- how osmolality was set (the plan does not state it);
- the residual and Cook's distance tests flag almost every correctly specified model on this design: an
  influence analysis as PCR-005 PD-005-02 does;
- the 16-run augmentation (S-BRX-AUG) was foreseen by "or the design augmented" (computed PD-003-02).
