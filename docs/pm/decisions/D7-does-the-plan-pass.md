# D7 — does a plan written under the rebuilt apparatus read as a paper?

**What is being asked.** `A.pdf` and `B.pdf`, both the whole `PCP-005` protocol (Protein A,
Step 5, about thirty pages). One is the shipped plan; the other was written in one pass by one
agent under `authoring/RUNNER.md` as rebuilt, then reviewed once on the four content questions.
Which is which is sealed in `blind-key-PCP-005.md`, drawn before the new one was written.

Why a plan: half the corpus is plans and the regime has been tested only on reports (`PCR-007`,
preferred blind on 2026-08-19). Plans are prospective, use `plan_params`, state no findings, and
already sit high on the passive (66.7 % in the shipped `PCP-005` against sources at 57–64 %) — a
different genre with different traps.

Suggested read, the same sections in each: Purpose and scope; Prior knowledge and quality risk
basis; Study design; Acceptance and decision criteria; Risks and assumptions. **Quote the sentences
that read as machine prose, with A or B; say which reads as a paper.** Recorded verbatim before the
key is opened.

**The rule, unchanged** (`state.json → decisions.pass_rule`): PASS iff you judge the new document
the better text *and* quote fewer than five sentences from it. Applied mechanically.

**Option A — PASS.** `PCP-005` is promoted and re-anchored (48 quotes, no spans), and the five
batches run: eighteen documents, each batch promoted as one serial annex step and one document of
it read by you blind (D8).

**Option B — FAIL.** The draft stays in the unit, nothing is promoted, the batches do not start, and
the results page records what you named — which is then the next thing to change before another
plan is tried.

**What the plan assumes meanwhile.** Nothing that depends on the answer runs before it.
