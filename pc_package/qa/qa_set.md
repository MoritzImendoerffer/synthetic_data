## SUM: summarization tasks

### `SUM-control-strategy`

**Q.** Summarize the control strategy for A-Mab drug substance across the process steps, as the Version 2.0 documents state it.

**A.** See key points.

- The batch record carries every CPP and WC-CPP, 21 in all, with set-point and NOR. (PTP-001)
- In-process limits bound what each intermediate may deliver to the next step. (PTP-001)
- At the bioreactor the four WC-CPPs and the CPP osmolality are held by the control system, with a combined alert and an acidic variants action limit at harvest. (PCR-003, PCR-003)
- Harvest has no CPP or WC-CPP; its two KPPs are held in their NORs and turbidity is an in-process control. (PCR-004, PCR-004)
- Host cell protein and DNA are controlled by their clearance at Protein A, cation exchange and anion exchange. (PCR-004)
- At Protein A, protein load and elution buffer pH are WC-CPPs. (PCR-005)
- At low-pH viral inactivation, inactivation pH is a CPP and hold time and temperature are WC-CPPs. (PCR-006, PCR-006)
- At virus filtration, filtration volume and pressure are both WC-CPPs. (PCR-009)
- The load conditions that each viral clearance claim depends on are controlled through the batch record. (PTP-001)
- Release testing confirms the drug substance attributes. (PTP-001)

### `SUM-criteria-derivation`

**Q.** Summarize how the intermediate acceptance criteria of Version 2.0 are derived and verified.

**A.** See key points.

- A drug substance criterion cannot judge an upstream pool, so each step output has an intermediate acceptance criterion. (PCMP-001)
- Each drug substance criterion is carried back through the platform worst case of each downstream step, with no margin. (PCMP-001, PCMP-001)
- Attributes that no downstream step changes keep the drug substance criterion (the glycans). (PCMP-001)
- Verification uses the one-sided 95 % prediction bound of a single batch, never a confidence bound of the mean. (PCMP-001)
- A worse A-Mab worst case tightens upstream criteria; a better bound widens them only if the model passes the first three adequacy criteria. (PCMP-001)
- Of 22 criteria, 17 stand at their a priori value and verification changed 5. (PCMP-001)
- UF/DF raises aggregate within its NOR, so every aggregate criterion carries that increase. (PCMP-001)
- Each change of a criterion is argued by its risk, in four parts. (PCMP-001)
- Residual DNA has no intermediate criterion and is judged on the drug substance only. (PCMP-001)

### `SUM-inv-002`

**Q.** Summarize investigation INV-002 on the response-surface models, its cause, remedy and outcome.

**A.** See key points.

- The Version 2.0 re-analysis found 14 of the 22 full quadratic models above the limit on overfitting. (INV-002)
- The three tests are a significant model F-test, no significant lack of fit and R² minus predicted R² of at most 0.20. (INV-002)
- Root cause, the plans prescribed the full quadratic model with no model selection, on designs small for it. (INV-002)
- No range was set from a failing model while the investigation was open. (INV-002)
- A remedy rule approved before any new run augments a failing model of a drug substance attribute that keeps an effect, and refits the others. (INV-002)
- Viral clearance models are refitted; no augmentation spikes virus. (INV-002)
- Both augmentations were run by 2026-09-24, and model selection (CA3) then replaced the refits for every output. (INV-002)
- The augmentation runs moved no criterion; the investigation is classed minor and closed on 2026-09-28. (INV-002, INV-002)
